# Retail_Sales_Customer_Analysis
This project analyzes retail sales performance, profitability, store/channel performance and customer behavior using 1,800 synthetic transaction records.
Business Problem
A retail business wants to understand:
Which categories generate the most sales and profit?
Which regions and stores perform best?
Which channels are most valuable?
How profitable are customers?
Which customers are Champions, Loyal, At Risk or Dormant?
How do discounts affect profitability?
How are sales and profit changing over time?

Objectives
Measure sales, profit and margin.
Analyze category, region, store and channel performance.
Understand customer-level revenue and profitability.
Perform RFM segmentation.
Analyze discount bands and profit margin.
Build an executive Excel dashboard.
Demonstrate SQL analytical queries.
Demonstrate a Python analysis workflow.
Convert analysis into business recommendations.

Tools Used
Excel – Dashboard, KPI reporting and visualization
SQL – Aggregations, segmentation and business queries
Python / Pandas – Cleaning, derived metrics and RFM analysis
GitHub – Portfolio presentation

RFM Analysis
RFM stands for:
Recency
How recently a customer purchased.
Frequency
How often a customer purchased.
Monetary
How much the customer spent.
The project combines the three dimensions to classify customers into segments such as:
Champions
Loyal Customers
New / Promising
Potential Loyalists
At Risk
Dormant

Key KPIs
Net Sales
`Gross Sales - Discount Amount`
Profit
`Net Sales - COGS`
Profit Margin
`Profit / Net Sales × 100`
Average Transaction Value
`Total Net Sales / Number of Transactions`
Analytical Workflow
Raw Data → Data Cleaning → KPI Calculation → Sales Analysis → Customer Analysis → RFM Segmentation → Business Insights
Business Recommendations
The analysis framework supports decisions such as:
Prioritize profitable categories, not only high-sales categories.
Use RFM segmentation to design retention and reactivation campaigns.
Target Champions and Loyal Customers with cross-sell and premium offers.
Reactivate At-Risk customers using personalized promotions.
Use discount guardrails to prevent unnecessary margin erosion.
Compare channels on both sales and profitability before increasing marketing investment.
Use store/region performance to identify operational practices worth replicating.
